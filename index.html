A framework for holistic thinking and planning

Plan with the whole system in mind

OCEANIS is a collaborative, non-prescriptive planning tool for integrated ocean action. It helps you ask questions, connect disciplines, and reflect across all aspects of a project, program, proposal, or other initiative.

Explore the framework →

Breaking siloed thinking

One objective. Many connected questions.

Use OCEANIS in workshops, proposals, projects, and programmes, at any stage and as often as useful. Visualised as a Spiderweb of interconnected components, the OCEANIS framework highlights that these components are interdependent rather than linear steps. This helps us understand relationships, identify gaps, and strengthen the overall effectiveness of our work.
The framework can be applied to projects, programmes, observing systems, or partnerships at any stage, and is relevant not only for ocean observing and biodiversity initiatives, but is designed to support any kind of work or collaborative efforts. The framework grew through a co-creative process with interdisciplinary contributors and is designed to support reflection, not prescribe a single route.
