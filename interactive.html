Explore the framework

OCEANIS interactive spiderweb

Note: This interactive tool is in active development.

OCEANIS is visualised by a Spiderweb with interconnected components. Each component consists of descriptor words, associated themes, and can be combined with question words to generate questions. We encourage you to explore components including the links between them.


Select a component or connection to explore its descriptors, themes, and question words.

Single Select

Use the Single or Multi-Select mode to focus on individual components or explore multiple connections at once.

Create questions by combining:

WHO WHERE WHEN WHAT WHY HOW
+
Descriptor words Themes of interest

Example: Exploration + How + Map + Knowledge landscape = “How do we map out the knowledge landscape?”
